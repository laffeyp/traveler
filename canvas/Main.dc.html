Factory UI — Vocabulary Reference

Every registered name a downstream artboard may cite.

read 2026-08-26 · from contracts/*.yaml and src/driver/visibility.ts · sprint 053, phase D.1

The registries under contracts/ are the project's locked vocabulary. Every artboard in this canvas cites names from those files — verbatim, never softened, never paraphrased. This sheet lists what the vocabulary contains today. A name absent from this sheet is absent from the UI.

Receiving-rule ids 10 · contracts/receiving-rules.yaml

Each rule id appears in ReceivingCheck.blockers[]. A blocker view cites the id and reads its description verbatim.

iddescription
certificate_of_conformance_presentThe consignment carries a certificate of conformance covering the delivered lot or serial.
certificate_of_conformance_expiredA certificate of conformance was presented but its expiry is past, missing or unparseable. Distinct from certificate_of_conformance_present: absent paperwork and stale paperwork are different facts.
certificate_of_conformance_unverifiedA certificate of conformance covering the delivered goods exists, but nobody has accepted it as evidence. Capturing records that paperwork arrived; it does not record that anyone read it.
material_test_report_presentRaw material carries a mill test report for the delivered heat or lot.
material_test_report_unverifiedA mill test report covering the delivered heat or lot exists but has not been verified.
first_article_report_presentA new part revision carries a first article report. Scoped to the part revision, not the lot.
first_article_report_unverifiedA first article report for the part revision exists but has not been verified.
process_certificate_presentA part that went through a special process at the supplier — heat treat, plating, NDT, welding — carries the certificate for that process against the delivered lot or serial.
process_certificate_unverifiedA process certificate covering the delivered lot exists but nobody has verified it.
document_matches_part_revisionA presented document names the part revision it is offered against.

Visibility levels 4 · src/driver/visibility.ts

The read-side outcomes of an access-aware record read. full and summary return a record shape; denied and hidden_existence return no record. §5.4 invariant: the bytes of hidden_existence match those of not-found.

levelwhat the caller receives
fullthe whole record; every field
summarya registered §10 shape, revealed fields only. Four shapes registered today, for MachineEvidenceRecord, Certificate, Nonconformance, GeneratedReport
deniedthe refusal and its reason code; no record data
hidden_existenceno record; the shape is byte-identical to a not-found response. The audit event captures the difference; the caller does not
