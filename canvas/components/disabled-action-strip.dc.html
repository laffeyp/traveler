Disabled action strip

sprint 054 · shared component · beside every refused action

Beside every disabled action, the strip names the specific reason (registered rule id or reason code), what condition would clear it, and which caller_type resolves. Never a generic "you can't do this".

Why disabled · certificate_of_conformance_unverified
Receiving-rule blocker on this line — a certificate is present but has not been accepted as evidence.
clears when a quality_engineer fires AcceptCertificateAsEvidence · rule receiving_decision · walks Certificate to verified · re-runs RunReceivingCheck
Why disabled · required_measurements_present
Run-close-rule blocker — a required step has no captured measurement.
clears when an operator fires CaptureMeasurement on the named step · rule run_execution · emits MEASUREMENT_CAPTURED
Why refused · authorization_denied · guard elevated_disposition_authority
RecordDisposition allows the caller (rule disposition_recording), but the disposition kind is use_as_is or repair. The disposition-kind guard evaluates the actor against elevated_disposition_authority and refuses this actor.
resolves when a manufacturing_engineer or quality_engineer records the disposition
Why disabled · support_context_missing
Reason code from an access decision — no open, in-scope, in-time SupportSession for this caller.
clears when an access_admin or support_user fires OpenSupportSession with a scope covering this record · rule support_session_management
