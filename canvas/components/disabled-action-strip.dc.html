Disabled action strip

sprint 054 · shared component · beside every refused action

Beside every disabled action, the strip names the specific reason (registered rule id or reason code), what condition would clear it, and which caller_type resolves. Never a generic "you can't do this".

Why disabled · certificate_of_conformance_unverified
Receiving-rule blocker on this line — a certificate is present but has not been accepted as evidence.
clears when a quality_engineer fires AcceptCertificateAsEvidence · rule receiving_decision · walks Certificate to verified · re-runs RunReceivingCheck
Why disabled · required_measurements_present
Run-close-rule blocker — a required step has no captured measurement.
clears when an operator fires CaptureMeasurement on the named step · rule run_execution · emits MEASUREMENT_CAPTURED
Why refused · authorization_denied · guard elevated_disposition_authority
RecordDisposition allows the caller (rule disposition_recording), but the disposition kind is use_as_is or repair. The disposition-kind guard evaluates the actor against elevated_disposition_authority and refuses this actor.
resolves when a manufacturing_engineer or quality_engineer records the disposition
Why disabled · support_context_missing
Reason code from an access decision — no open, in-scope, in-time SupportSession for this caller.
clears when an access_admin or support_user fires OpenSupportSession with a scope covering this record · rule support_session_management

Physical Presence refusals (Phase G)

Why disabled · presentation_expired
The bound Presentation's expires_at has passed. Handlers evaluate world.clock >= Presentation.expires_at at read time via the presentationExpired helper.
clears when the operator re-scans and fires PresentInventoryAtStation · rule physical_presence · creates a fresh Presentation
Why refused · wrong_item
The presented item's inventory_item_id does not match the install's child_inventory_alias. Thrown from InstallInventory's bound-item check (a second throw sits in BindPresentedItemToRunStep's expected-child check when the operation input carries both parent and expected-child fields).
clears when the operator re-scans the correct child and fires PresentInventoryAtStation plus BindPresentedItemToRunStep
Why refused · state_transition_forbidden (F2b first-class parent)
Runtime-executor throw from world.ts moveState when no state-machine transition matches the operation and the record's current state. Grey because the throw is executor-level; the specific state that failed is named in the message.
clears when the record advances to a state the operation's state machine permits — for InventoryItem into InstallInventory, that's reserved, kitted, or in_wip
Why refused · idempotency_conflict (F2b first-class parent)
Runtime-executor throw from driver.ts on a same-key different-tuple call to a transactional_unique_constraint operation, or on a duplicate write-boundary key. The tuple shape lives on contracts/operations.yaml:idempotency_tuple_fields.
clears when the caller mints a fresh idempotency_key for the intended new tuple — same-key retries of the identical call return the memoised result and are not a conflict
