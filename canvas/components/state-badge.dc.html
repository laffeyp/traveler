Terminal states carry the strike-through and a mute; the record cannot leave them. Colour uses: red for refusal states (blocked, quarantined, close_blocked); amber for waiting-on-review (paused, review_required); green for the load-bearing success state ONLY — the terminal success step of a lifecycle (closed, verified). Grey for ground including working states (in_progress); strike-through grey for other terminal states that are not a success (cancelled, rejected, scrapped). Philosophy §3.1: green is finite; if every step were green nothing would draw the eye.
