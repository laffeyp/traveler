Empty and no-authority states

Eleven reusable patterns. Every list, queue, search, or detail surface picks from this set when the result is empty or the caller cannot act.

sprint 056 · phase D.1 · §8 of the design spec

A blank list without a named cause shows a claim the surface did not check. Every pattern below names its cause and cites the registered reason code, rule id, or condition that would clear it.
